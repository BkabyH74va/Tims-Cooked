  What I just found (important)

  The previous assumption that corruption was firing was wrong. Over HTTP the
  two trigger stages coalesce into one decoder input and decode cleanly (5164 B,
  verified locally at 1K–64K chunk sizes). The bug needs stage 2 in a later
  decoder call. The server now sends stage 1, pauses 300 ms with TCP_NODELAY,
  then stage 2 — that's the fix, and the oracle will show it immediately if it
  works.
